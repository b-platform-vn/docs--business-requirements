# Platform Ecosystem Hub

Welcome to the internal documentation for our company's entire technology and business platform ecosystem.

This documentation serves as the single source of truth for:
1. **Business Units & Products**: How our organization works and what domains we serve.
2. **Tools & Apps**: What systems and platforms make up each business unit's infrastructure.
3. **Features & Capabilities**: The exact technical and functional capabilities of each component under those applications.

## How to Navigate

- **[Product Overview](/products/)**: High-level mapping of all our business units.
- **Product Hubs**: Deep-dive into specific products from the sidebar.
- **Application features**: Select individual apps underneath the products to see exactly what they do.
