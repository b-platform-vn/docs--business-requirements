## The Problem

Enterprise Business customers must provide and maintain company details (name, tax ID, registration info) for B2B invoicing, compliance, and tax purposes. Without self-service company info management, updates require support intervention.

- B2B invoicing requires verified company information.
- Company details change (name, tax ID, representative).
- Compliance mandates accurate business entity records.

## Proposed Solution

A **Company Information Management** section for Enterprise Business accounts to view and update their company details.

### Goals

- Self-service company info updates.
- Ensure accurate data for invoicing/compliance.
- Reduce support overhead.

### Out-of-scope

- Company verification / KYB (future).
- Individual Business accounts (they don't have company entities).

### Measurable Outcomes

- Company info completion rate at registration.
- Support tickets for company info changes.

## Requirements

### Company Profile

- [P0] View company name, tax ID, registration number.
- [P0] Edit company details.
- [P0] Upload business registration documents.
- [P1] Company logo upload.
- [P1] Industry / business type selection.

### Invoicing

- [P0] Company info populates order invoices.
- [P1] Separate billing entity details if different from company.
