## The Problem

Business customers need to view and update their personal account information (name, contact details, preferences) without contacting support.

- Account details change over time (phone, email, name).
- Self-service reduces support burden.

## Proposed Solution

A **Profile Management** section where authenticated users can view and edit their personal information.

### Goals

- Self-service account info updates.
- Reduce support tickets for basic profile changes.

### Out-of-scope

- Company information (see Company Information Management).
- Address management (see Addresses Management).

### Measurable Outcomes

- Profile edit success rate.
- Reduction in profile-related support tickets.

## Requirements

### View / Edit Profile

- [P0] View current profile (name, email, phone).
- [P0] Edit name and phone.
- [P1] Change email (with verification flow).
- [P1] Avatar / profile photo upload.
- [P2] Notification preferences.
