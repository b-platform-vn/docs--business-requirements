## The Problem

Business customers may miss promotional campaigns, seasonal deals, or platform events unless there is a dedicated section surfacing current and upcoming activities.

- Promotions drive bulk orders but are invisible without a dedicated space.
- Customers want to know about events, campaigns, and new product launches.

## Proposed Solution

An **Activities** page listing promotions, campaigns, events, and news relevant to business customers.

### Goals

- Increase awareness and engagement with promotions.
- Drive additional bulk orders through time-limited campaigns.

### Out-of-scope

- Personalized promotion targeting.
- Coupon/voucher code system (separate feature if needed).

### Measurable Outcomes

- CTR from Activities page to product/quote actions.
- Campaign participation rate.

## Requirements

### Activity Listing

- [P0] Display list of current/upcoming activities.
- [P0] Each activity shows title, date range, brief description, image.
- [P1] Filter by type (promotion, event, news).
- [P1] Activity detail page with full content.

### Management

- [P1] Admin-managed content (CMS or admin panel).
- [P2] Scheduled publish/expire dates.
