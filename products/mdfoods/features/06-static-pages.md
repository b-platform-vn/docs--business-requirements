## The Problem

Business customers need access to company information, policies, and guides to build trust, understand purchasing terms, and resolve common questions before placing bulk orders.

- New customers need to verify legitimacy and understand how the platform works.
- Policies (delivery, returns, payment) must be transparent for B2B compliance.
- Reducing support tickets by providing self-service information.

## Proposed Solution

A set of **Static Pages** covering company information, policies, and guides — all accessible from the footer and main navigation.

### Goals

- Build customer trust through transparency.
- Reduce support inquiries by surfacing answers proactively.
- Meet B2B compliance requirements (policy disclosures).

### Out-of-scope

- Dynamic/personalized content on static pages.
- CMS-level editing by non-developers (future).

### Measurable Outcomes

- Reduction in support tickets for policy-related questions.
- Page views on key policy pages before first order.
