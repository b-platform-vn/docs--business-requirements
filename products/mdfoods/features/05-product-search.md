## The Problem

Business customers with specific product needs want to find items quickly without manually browsing through categories. A search function reduces friction for returning customers who already know what they want.

- Bulk buyers often know the product name or SKU and want direct access.
- Browsing categories is inefficient for repeat orders.

## Proposed Solution

A **Product Search** feature allowing customers to search products across all categories by keyword, SKU, or attributes with autocomplete support.

### Goals

- Reduce time-to-find for known products.
- Support search by name, SKU, or attribute keyword.

### Out-of-scope

- AI-powered recommendations.
- Faceted search beyond basic filters.

### Measurable Outcomes

- Search success rate (searches resulting in a product view).
- Average time from search to cart-add.

## Requirements

### Search

- [P0] Global search input accessible from header.
- [P0] Search by product name, SKU, description keyword.
- [P0] Display results with product image, name, price.
- [P1] Autocomplete / suggestions as user types.

### Results

- [P0] Paginated results list.
- [P1] Filter results by category.
- [P1] Sort results by relevance, price, name.
- [P2] "No results" state with suggestions.
