# MDFoods — Overview

> B2B Food Supplement Online platform.

**Category:** Customer-facing

## Mission

MDFoods is a **web page** where business customers — **Individual Businesses** or **Enterprise Businesses** — can get **quoted prices** and **order large amounts** of food supplement products to secure better pricing. It focuses purely on the B2B storefront and quote-to-order experience, relying on other platforms for customer care and product data.

## Role & Responsibility

| Area | Description |
|---|---|
| Category | Customer-facing |
| Domain | B2B Food Supplement |
| Form Factor | Web page only (no mobile / native app) |
| Users | Individual Business customers, Enterprise Business customers |
| Key Outcome | Enable business customers to request quotes and place bulk orders at negotiated/volume pricing |

## What MDFoods Does

- Present food supplement products to business customers on the web
- Let customers **request a quote** based on the products and quantities they need
- Provide **volume-based / negotiated pricing** — larger orders get better prices
- Allow customers to **place bulk orders** once a quote is accepted
- Support two customer types: **Individual Business** and **Enterprise Business**

## Dependencies

MDFoods is a thin storefront. It depends on two other platforms in the ecosystem:

| Depends On | Category | What It Provides to MDFoods |
|---|---|---|
| [CRM](/products/crm/) | Back-office | Takes care of MDFoods' customers — customer records, communication, and support. |
| Product Management System (PMS) | Back-office | Provides product metadata: name, SKU, description, price, and other product attributes. |
