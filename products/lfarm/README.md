# LFarm — Overview

> Organic Farm customer-facing website.

**Category:** Customer-facing

## Mission

LFarm is a **web page** where customers and partners can learn about LFarm's organic farming mission, explore farm activities and articles, and find reliable contact and legal information. It focuses on public-facing content, brand trust, and customer/partner engagement.

## Role & Responsibility

| Area | Description |
|---|---|
| Category | Customer-facing |
| Domain | Organic Farming |
| Form Factor | Web page only (no mobile / native app) |
| Users | End consumers (customers), Partners |
| Key Outcome | Help customers and partners understand LFarm, discover organic-farming content, and contact the business through accurate public information |

## What LFarm Does

- Present LFarm's organic farming brand, mission, and activities on the web
- Publish articles/news about farming, products, activities, and community updates
- Let users browse activity/news content by locale
- Provide accurate contact information and social links
- Provide legal pages such as **Terms and Conditions** and **Data Privacy**

## Dependencies

LFarm is a public content website. It uses the same customer-facing stack as MDFoods but does **not** depend on a Product Management System (PMS).

| Depends On | Category | What It Provides to LFarm |
|---|---|---|
| [CRM](/products/crm/) | Back-office | Supports customer communication and follow-up when users contact LFarm. |
